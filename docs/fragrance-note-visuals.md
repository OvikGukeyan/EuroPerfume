# Fragrance note visuals

The note pyramid uses the catalog’s saved note metadata. Do not guess unpublished pyramids or copy another scent’s notes. Brands that do not disclose note pyramids (including Xerjoff Join the Club) should remain without one.

Existing storefront note material photos remain in the original atlas. Reviewed labels (RU and DE) can link to the established picture for the same locally recognizable material. Geography, variety and extraction wording remain as supplied; a shared illustration is not evidence of a production site, extraction or INCI. The updated lookup matches normalized labels rather than database IDs.

`materials-01.webp` and `materials-02.webp` are original, generated photorealistic illustrations created for this catalog; they are not third-party product or botanical-source photography. Some accord images are intentionally symbolic. Keep the pictures decorative and display the exact note names separately.

For future imports, check saved top/heart/base notes against official material, confirm every disclosed note has an appropriate mapped visual, and spot-check both languages. An unknown note displays its label alone until a verified visual or intentional same-material alias is added. Avoid substring matching, unrelated placeholders, and third-party images without verified reuse rights. Run `npx tsx --test tests/fragrance-note-visual.test.ts` after adding or altering a mapping.
